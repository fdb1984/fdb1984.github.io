More than a migraine diary.

The most useful guidance from the support and privacy pages, organised alongside the product.

01

Quick logging

Start an attack in a few taps and add details later when convenient. The diary, calendar, medication, and basic report remain free.

02

Personal signals

Once enough recent entries are available, changes, medication effect, recurrence within 24 hours, and monthly differences become easier to understand.

03

During a consultation

Use a personal checklist and keep notes about medication, examinations, and follow-up.

04

Health, weather, and widgets

Apple Health, Health Connect, watches, local weather, and air pressure are optional. Widgets bring key information and quick access to your home screen.

05

Private on your device

No ads, no sale of health data, and no diary server. Appointment notes are processed locally without an external AI service.

06

Export and recovery

Keep an export yourself. Android can restore an encrypted backup through your Google account; local iOS app files are excluded from device backups.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

01Do I need to enter something every day?+
A

No. Focus mainly on attacks, medication, and days when something noteworthy happens.

02Can I use the app with my doctor?+
A

Yes. The report and checklist help you bring the most important points into the conversation.

03Can anyone read my appointment notes?+
A

The maker cannot view them. The content stays local and can be protected with biometrics or your device passcode.

04How do I manage Premium?+
A

Purchase, restore, and cancellation are handled by the App Store or Google Play, which always shows the current price and terms.

A layout for every place.

The practical capabilities from the full support page, brought together in a clear overview.

01

Wall panel or remote

Use an iPad in landscape as a fixed panel, or an iPhone in portrait as a quick remote for your HomeKit home.

02

What matters at home

Time, weather, rain forecast, climate, lights, doors, shades, sensors, and alerts only appear when relevant data is available.

03

Search and control

Find scenes, lights, switches, cameras, and sensors directly. Results show the current state first, with frequent actions ranked higher.

04

Boards for each place

Create separate boards for the living room, kitchen, or iPhone. Choose the visibility, order, and favourites for each one.

05

Cameras and alerts

Open cameras fullscreen and see clear alerts for low batteries, offline cameras, smoke alarms, or water leaks.

06

No separate account

The app does not require an extra account or send HomeKit data to an external app server.

FAQ

Good to know

01What do I need to get started?+
A

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later and an existing Apple HomeKit home. The app will request the access it needs.

02Can I create different boards?+
A

Yes. Boards let you choose which rooms, accessories, cameras, sensors, and panels appear in each place.

03Where does my data stay?+
A

HomeKit data stays in Apple HomeKit. App preferences can be stored through iCloud inside your Apple account.

04Is there a subscription?+
A

No. You can try the full app for 30 days and then unlock it with one one-time purchase through Apple.
