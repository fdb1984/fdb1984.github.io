Questions about HomeScreen for HomeKit

What you need, what you can control and how the purchase works.

What is HomeScreen for HomeKit?

HomeScreen for HomeKit is an iPad and iPhone dashboard and control app by de Bruin Studio. See time, weather and home status, and control your selected lights, scenes and accessories. It uses your existing home in Apple's Home app; it does not replace HomeKit setup or automation editing.

Can I turn my iPad into a HomeKit control panel?

Yes. Place a compatible iPad on a table or wall and choose which rooms, scenes and devices appear. The iPad dashboard is designed for landscape use and supports Guided Access. On iPhone, you can also use portrait mode. Check the App Store for current device and system requirements.

Does HomeScreen work with my lights, Shelly devices or other smart devices?

HomeScreen reads devices and scenes available in your Apple Home. A device must be added to that home through HomeKit or a suitable bridge. An account with Hue, Shelly or Home Assistant alone is not enough. Available controls depend on what the device exposes through HomeKit; check your own home during the trial.

Can I run HomeKit scenes from CarPlay?

Yes. Choose up to four CarPlay favourites per home on your iPhone, independently of your dashboard favourites. A compatible car or head unit is required. Apple may require confirmation or an unlocked iPhone for secure actions such as opening a garage or lock. HomeScreen does not bypass that protection.

Are there widgets for favourites and alerts?

Yes. Home Screen widgets let you control favourites and view home alerts. You can switch homes in the favourites widget. Widget status updates periodically; these are not continuously updated camera feeds.

Does HomeScreen require a subscription?

No. Try all features for 30 days, then unlock the full version with a one-time in-app purchase through Apple, including Family Sharing. There is no automatic charge when the trial ends. The in-app purchase shows the current price for your country.

Is my HomeKit home stored on a separate server?

No. There is no separate HomeScreen account, advertising tracking or proprietary home cloud at de Bruin Studio. Dashboards and shared settings can be saved to iCloud using your Apple account. Favourites and idle-screen behaviour remain per device. This is not a backup of your HomeKit home or automations.

Where can I find the guide and support?

Read the HomeScreen guide for settings, cameras, visibility, iCloud and purchase help. HomeScreen for HomeKit is developed by de Bruin Studio. For individual help, email support@dbruin.com.
