Privacy in DroneGlance.

Your drone profile and saved places are stored on your device. Maps, searches, weather, purchases and advertising involve the providers explained below.

Updated on 26 November 2026

This policy describes version 1.0.6. It does not state when that version is available in your store. The earlier Dutch policies are archived for iPhone through 1.0.5 and Android through 1.0.5.

DroneGlance is made by de Bruin Studio. The free version of version 1.0.6 uses one supported country with advertising. Premium is optional and removes advertising. For advertising, we use Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform. Read how Google processes personal information and our advertising explanation.

Your profile, places and free country

DroneGlance stores your drone selection, weight and relevant camera, toy, registration and training choices, display preferences, chosen free country, saved places and plans on your device. Saved places can contain a name, coordinates, country, planned time and height. You do not enter an operator registration number or upload a copy of a licence.

There is no separate DroneGlance user account or studio-operated map server. de Bruin Studio does not operate a server that receives your drone profile, collection of saved places or location history. This does not mean that external providers receive no data: their requests and technical processing are described below.

Choose and confirm your fixed free home country once. The choice is stored on your device and does not change automatically when you search, travel or use your location. Other supported countries require Premium. A new installation of version 1.0.6 does not start a new trial. An already-started, still-valid trial keeps only its remaining time with all supported countries and no advertising. When that period or paid access ends, you can continue with one free country and advertising.

The app temporarily stores downloaded source and weather data to avoid unnecessary requests and to show previously loaded information when a connection fails. These caches can reveal areas you previously viewed. Cached information keeps its original date; storing it does not make it current or complete.

To respect the weather provider’s waiting times, the app keeps a small local record of requested weather positions and when another request is allowed. This includes limited coordinates and request or waiting times. It is separate from the forecast cache and is not a continuous route. A request attempt does not become a newly validated forecast. The bounded record is cleaned up when elapsed waiting periods are processed during later requests; it is not a promise of deletion at an exact clock instant.

Using your current location

You choose whether to grant location permission. While you use the app, it can obtain a current position to select the spot to check. You can instead search for a place or select a point on the map. DroneGlance does not record a continuous GPS route or use background location tracking for these place checks.

A device may supply an approximate location. That can be too imprecise near a zone boundary, so check that the map point is your actual take-off location. You can change the app’s location permission in your device settings. Location permission and advertising privacy choices are separate controls.

iPhone: maps, weather, sources and purchases

Apple Maps and searching

Apple supplies the base map and the search for addresses and place names. Apple processes the search text or coordinates used to search for or identify your chosen spot. These are requests for the map and search features, not advertising-targeting fields supplied by DroneGlance.

Apple Weather and MET Norway

Apple Weather receives your selected coordinates through WeatherKit to provide a forecast. If Apple Weather is unavailable, the app requests a forecast directly from MET Norway using coordinates limited to four decimal places. This still identifies a precise place. Your drone profile is not included.

MET Norway receives your IP address and logs the request, which can contain coordinates. Its API terms describe access logs stored in Norway; its privacy policy explains the processing. The app identifies the weather provider and time, caches forecasts, and updates this weather feature while you use the app.

The source countries in this version

The iPhone version connects to source maps for the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, Denmark, Finland, France, Luxembourg, Portugal, Spain, Sweden and Switzerland. Source availability differs by country. The wider country directory is not a promise of connected source maps for every listed country.

For the Netherlands, Finland, Portugal and Sweden, the app downloads a published national file without including your selected map area. For Belgium, Germany, Denmark, France, Luxembourg, Spain and Switzerland, requests contain the small map area around your selected spot. Spain uses ENAIRE servAIS. The providers and their network suppliers receive the connection information, including an IP address.

For the Netherlands, the app checks the Ministry of Infrastructure and Water Management’s publication on Veilig drone vliegen and downloads the linked file from the ministry’s public Amazon Web Services S3 storage. The ministry’s website and Amazon Web Services receive technical connection information. This file request does not include your selected coordinates or drone profile.

For Belgium, requests also cover publicly published Belgian NOTAM areas for the requested area and time. Additional public area conditions are retrieved through the map’s guest access, without a personal pilot account. Those requests may identify the relevant area. Your profile and collection of saved places are not submitted.

For other countries, this version does not download zone maps or conditions. You can open the authority’s information yourself. The app identifies its connected sources; their own policies govern their request logs and further processing.

Existing trial data and Apple purchases

The timing record for a still-valid earlier trial is kept in the device’s non-synchronising Keychain. It is not sent to de Bruin Studio or synchronised through iCloud by the app. The record can remain after deleting and reinstalling on the same device. Version 1.0.6 does not create a new trial record when you start.

Apple processes a subscription only when you confirm a purchase through the App Store. The app checks Apple-signed purchase information on your device to determine access. de Bruin Studio does not receive payment-card details and has no subscription account database or payment server. Restore and manage purchases using the same Apple account. Deleting the app does not cancel a purchased subscription.

See Apple’s privacy policy and Apple’s subscription management instructions.

Android: maps, weather, sources and purchases

Device geocoding and Photon

Address searches and identification of your selected place use your Android device’s geocoding service. It may process your search text or selected coordinates over the internet. Its provider depends on the device and Android installation; it is not a search server operated by de Bruin Studio.

If that service gives no result, fails or does not respond, the app tries Photon by komoot. Photon then receives your search text or the exact coordinates used to identify a place, together with your IP address. You do not need a personal komoot account. The provider may keep request logs to operate its service; see the public Photon service.

DroneGlance does not keep a permanent search-history list. Temporary Photon results are cached in app memory, with at most 50 cached requests. A result is reused only while it is less than one day old. A place is added to Saved places only when you save it. A cache reduces repeat requests; it does not remove an earlier provider log.

MapLibre and OpenFreeMap

MapLibre renders the base map. The map tiles, fonts and map images are requested from OpenFreeMap. Tile requests reveal the area and zoom level you are viewing. OpenFreeMap and its network suppliers receive connection information, including your IP address.

OpenFreeMap’s privacy policy describes limited access, error and security logs and possible use of Cloudflare. It says regular access logs do not include IP addresses, while error or security logs can. The app draws source zones over this base map.

MET Norway weather

The Android app requests forecasts directly from MET Norway. A request contains the coordinates of your selected spot, limited to four decimal places. This remains a precise location. It does not include your drone profile or collection of saved places.

MET receives your IP address and logs requests that can contain coordinates in Norway, as explained in its API terms and privacy policy. Forecasts are temporarily cached on your device and updated while you use the app. This weather feature does not run background forecast updates.

Three connected source countries

This Android version connects to source maps for the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany. It does not download the additional iPhone source countries.

For the Netherlands, it checks the Ministry’s Veilig drone vliegen publication and downloads the linked national file from public Amazon Web Services S3 storage. The ministry’s website and Amazon Web Services receive technical connection information, including your IP address. The file request contains no selected coordinates or drone profile.

For Belgium, the official sources receive the small map area and requested time window for drone zones and Belgian NOTAM areas. Additional public area conditions use Droneguide guest access, without a personal pilot account. For Germany, the dipul service on uas-betrieb.de receives the small map area around your selected spot. These requests do not submit your drone profile or collection of saved places.

The providers may retain connection and request data to operate and secure their services, diagnose errors or produce usage statistics. A stored or hashed map request is not automatically anonymous.

Existing trial data, review access and Google Play

A still-valid earlier trial uses the existing local timing record and clock checks. Those records are not sent to de Bruin Studio. Clearing Android app data or reinstalling can erase them. Version 1.0.6 does not start a new trial.

If a store reviewer enters a review-access code, it is checked on the device and only its hash is stored. This is separate from a Google Play subscription; the app does not create an account or send the code to a studio server.

Google Play processes subscriptions you confirm, payments and subscription management. The app receives and checks signed purchase information, such as the product, purchase status and purchase proof, to determine access. Google Play Billing also processes technical app, device and network information, purchase events, results, error and timing measurements, and a temporary session identifier. Some of that technical information may be queued locally before transmission.

DroneGlance receives no payment-card details and operates no payment server. Your Google Play account is not a DroneGlance account. Restore purchases with the same Google account. Manage or cancel a subscription through Google Play; deleting the app does not cancel it. Apple and Google Play manage purchases separately.

See Google’s privacy policy and Google Play’s subscription instructions.

Google AdMob and advertising privacy

The free version can show a Google AdMob banner. We use Google User Messaging Platform, or UMP, to handle advertising privacy choices. The app requests non-personalized ads. Your privacy choices can restrict ad serving further. Read Google Business Data Responsibility for Google’s use of personal information.

For ad delivery, measurement and security, Google may process your IP address, an approximate region inferred from it, technical device or SDK identifiers, ads shown, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Non-personalized does not mean no data processing or no technical identifiers.

In the advertising requests we make, we turn off automatic collection of your device’s approximate position by the advertising SDK and disable the publisher first-party ID. We do not include your selected flying coordinates, drone profile, search text, collection of saved places or an account number as advertising-targeting fields. Google still receives an IP address through the connection, and other technical SDK identifiers may be processed.

The requests to map, search, source and weather providers described above remain necessary for those features. Those providers are separate from the advertising-partner choices displayed by UMP. A Google advertising choice is not a choice about every map or weather provider.

If your preferences do not allow ordinary ad serving, Google may show an eligible limited ad or no ad. Programmatic limited ads can use cookies or local storage solely to detect invalid traffic and prevent fraud and abuse. IP address processing is still needed for the connection. See Google’s limited-ad explanation. Refusing a purpose is not replaced with permission to use it.

The account setting for sharing full IP addresses with ad buyers is off. This does not prevent Google from receiving an IP address while handling internet requests.

de Bruin Studio can view aggregate AdMob statistics, such as impressions and revenue.

Advertising software can retain technical advertising or error information for later processing. On Android, such information can be temporarily stored on the device for later processing or transmission. A banner disappearing or a switch to Premium does not automatically erase information already stored or sent. The providers’ policies explain their further processing and retention.

Google’s privacy policy and explanation for sites and apps using Google services provide further information. The SDKs’ data disclosures are available for iOS and Android.

Managing your choices

The app uses Google’s privacy message when needed and stores the relevant privacy choices on your device. UMP also sends requests to Google to handle this process. You can reopen the advertising privacy choices through the app’s Privacy or advertising-choices screen when those options are available. You can change or withdraw choices there.

Your free country’s map, conditions and weather remain usable if an ad is limited, unavailable or cannot be requested. A privacy choice does not change the legal conditions for flying and does not require buying Premium. A valid subscription or still-valid old trial provides ad-free use.

You can separately deny location access and search for a place, choose your fixed home country if you have not yet confirmed it, edit your profile, remove saved places or manage a purchased subscription in its store. These are separate actions; changing one does not automatically perform the others.

External links and contacting us

If you open a source, website or contact link, the browser, mail app or website handles the next step under its own policy. DroneGlance does not automatically register a flight or send an email when you view a source or contact option.

If you email de Bruin Studio, we receive your email address and what you send so we can handle your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information or exact private locations unless needed. External providers have their own policies for request logs, retention, processing locations and privacy requests.

Deleting data and exercising your rights

Remove saved places in the app and edit your drone profile through its profile controls. On iPhone, deleting the app removes its ordinary profile, saved-place and cache storage, but the old trial Keychain record may remain. “Offload App” preserves app data for later reinstallation. Device backups are governed by your iOS and iCloud settings.

On Android, you can clear local app data in Settings → Apps → DroneGlance → Storage; names vary by device. Uninstalling removes the local app storage. The app does not request cloud backup or device transfer of these local records.

Local deletion does not automatically remove earlier request logs or other records held by Google, Apple, MET Norway or the other providers, and does not cancel a subscription. Use the provider’s privacy and account tools for its records and the store’s subscription tools for payments.

For questions about information held by de Bruin Studio, contact info@debruinstudio.nl. You can ask for access, correction or deletion and, where applicable, restriction, portability or to object to processing. If processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it. Withdrawal does not by itself erase records already processed. Data held only on your device must be managed there; the studio does not receive those local records. Read more about privacy rights from the European Data Protection Board.

You can also raise a privacy concern with the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, the Dutch data protection authority, or your relevant data protection authority.
