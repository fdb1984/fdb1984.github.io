A migraine diary for iPhone and Android

MigraineMaatje is a migraine and headache diary app for recording attacks, medication and recovery. Start for free and create a basic report for your doctor or neurologist.

Devices
iPhone, iPad and Android, with a companion Apple Watch app.
Languages
Dutch, English, German and French.
Free basics
Recording and finishing attacks, diary, calendar, medication, basic status, medication days and a basic report.
Optional Premium
Personal explanations, medication effect, monthly analysis and appointment preparation. No subscription is needed to keep your diary.
Without a smartwatch
Yes. Record attacks and medication manually. Apple Health and Health Connect are optional.
Privacy
No ads and no sale of health data. You choose which connections and backup options to use.
